# Manuel Arce

I work at the intersection of semiconductors, AI infrastructure, and research automation.

Most of what I build starts with the same instinct: take a messy technical domain, turn it into something structured, auditable, and actually useful. That usually means a mix of deep industry research, OSINT workflows, data tooling, and systems work.

## What I Do

- Engineering-driven semiconductor research
- OSINT and evidence-heavy investigation pipelines
- Structured knowledge systems, databases, and internal research tools
- AI infrastructure work around GPUs, inference systems, and agent workflows
- Performance engineering for `vLLM`, `Slurm` jobs, and GPU-heavy workloads

## Things I Like Working On

- Semiconductor supply chains, fabs, packaging, memory, optics, and datacenter hardware
- Turning fragmented public information into clean datasets and usable research systems
- Building tools that help answer hard questions faster instead of just generating more text
- Low-latency, high-throughput inference systems
- Scheduler-aware GPU performance tuning and deployment ergonomics
- Weird crossovers between finance, hardware, infrastructure, and applied AI

## How I Think

I care a lot about first-principles analysis, source quality, and causal explanations.

If a system is slow, I want to know where the time goes.
If a claim is important, I want an evidence trail.
If a domain is complex, I want a model of how it actually works under the hood.

That mindset shows up across everything I touch: semiconductor analysis, fab mapping, Chinese/Taiwanese corporate research, GPU inference tuning, internal knowledge tooling, and visualization.
